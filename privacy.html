1. Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to DealMind AI ("we," "our," or "us"), a B2B Contract & Procurement Negotiation Co-Pilot SaaS platform. DealMind AI is developed and operated for enterprise B2B use cases, including contract analysis, vendor negotiation support, and procurement intelligence.

This policy governs data collected through:

  • The DealMind AI web platform (dealmind.ai)
  • API integrations with enterprise systems (ERP, CLM, Procurement platforms)
  • Email and support communications
  • HackwithHyderabad 3.0 demonstration environments

By using DealMind AI, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this Privacy Policy. Enterprise customers are also subject to the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) executed at onboarding.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Contract & Document Data

When you upload contracts or procurement documents, we process:

  • Document text content (contract clauses, terms, SLA specifications)
  • Vendor names, pricing figures, and negotiation parameters
  • Document metadata (filename, upload timestamp, file size)
  • Clause annotations and user-generated highlights

2.2 Negotiation History & Memory Data (Hindsight Memory)

Our Hindsight Memory feature collects and stores:

  • Historical negotiation outcomes and vendor concession records
  • SLA breach logs and performance history per vendor
  • Price escalation patterns and benchmark comparisons
  • Counter-offer drafts generated by the AI system
  • User feedback on AI suggestions (thumbs up/down, edits)

2.3 Account & Usage Data

  • Enterprise account credentials (email, SSO tokens — passwords are hashed)
  • User roles and permissions within your organization
  • Feature usage analytics (pages visited, buttons clicked, time spent)
  • Device information, browser type, IP address (anonymized after 30 days)
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

2.4 Communication Data

  • Support ticket content and correspondence
  • Feature requests and feedback submissions
  • Onboarding session recordings (with explicit consent)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Analyzing contracts, generating risk scores, and producing AI-powered counter-offers
  • Hindsight Memory: Building and maintaining your organization's negotiation memory database to improve leverage over time
  • Risk Detection: Identifying high-risk clauses, SLA vulnerabilities, and pricing anomalies
  • Product Improvement: Improving AI model accuracy using aggregated, anonymized data only (you may opt out)
  • Security: Detecting fraud, unauthorized access, and protecting platform integrity
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting regulatory obligations under GDPR, CCPA, SOC2, and applicable enterprise procurement regulations

We do not use your data for advertising, data brokerage, or sale to third parties under any circumstances.

4. AI & Machine Learning Processing

DealMind AI uses Large Language Models (LLMs) and proprietary machine learning to analyze contracts and generate counter-offers. You should know:

4.1 Zero-Retention Inference

AI inference calls (contract analysis, counter-offer generation) are processed using zero-retention APIs. Your contract text is not stored by our AI inference providers after processing is complete. We use enterprise-tier API agreements that prohibit training on customer data.

4.2 Hindsight Memory Training (Opt-In Only)

Your organizational negotiation data may be used to fine-tune your dedicated memory model with explicit opt-in consent only. This creates a private, isolated memory namespace for your organization — never shared across tenants.

4.3 Aggregate Anonymized Insights

We may use de-identified, aggregated benchmarks (e.g., "average SaaS price increase across platform: 18%") to improve general platform intelligence. Individual contract data is never used in aggregate benchmarks without anonymization.

4.4 AI Output Disclaimer

AI-generated counter-offers, risk scores, and recommendations are advisory only and do not constitute legal advice. All AI outputs must be reviewed by qualified legal and procurement professionals before use in live negotiations.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption at Rest: All contract data encrypted with AES-256 in dedicated encrypted storage volumes
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 enforced for all data transmission; HSTS headers applied
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC), MFA enforcement, audit logging on all data access
  • Infrastructure: SOC2 Type II compliant cloud infrastructure; regional data residency options available for EU and APAC customers
  • Penetration Testing: Annual third-party penetration testing; results available under NDA to enterprise customers
  • Incident Response: 72-hour breach notification commitment in compliance with GDPR Article 33
  • Backup & Recovery: Automated encrypted backups with 99.99% availability SLA; point-in-time recovery within 30 days

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • AI Inference Providers: Contract text is transmitted to AI API providers under zero-retention agreements. Providers: OpenAI (enterprise tier), Anthropic (enterprise tier). Both operate under GDPR-compliant DPAs.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS (primary), with data processed in your selected region
  • Security & Compliance Tools: Datadog (ironic but true) for infrastructure monitoring; Sentry for error tracking — only telemetry data, never contract content
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data when required by law, court order, or valid government request, with prompt notification to affected customers where legally permitted

We never sell, rent, or trade personal or business data to data brokers, advertisers, or market research firms.

7. Cookie Policy

Required for the platform to function. These cannot be disabled:

  • dm_csrf — Cross-site request forgery protection (expires: session)
  • dealmind_cookie_prefs — Your cookie consent preferences (expires: 1 year)

Used to understand how users interact with the platform to improve features. Requires consent:

  • dm_perf — Page load performance metrics (expires: 30 days)

7.3 Managing Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences at any time via our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent platform access. You may also clear all cookies via your browser to reset preferences.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR / CCPA §1798.110): Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR / CCPA §1798.105): Request deletion of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Export your negotiation memory data in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
  • Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): Object to AI training on your data or processing for analytics
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): Limit how we use your data in specific circumstances
  • Opt-Out of Sale (CCPA §1798.120): We do not sell data; this right is always honored
  • Non-Discrimination (CCPA §1798.125): Exercising privacy rights will not affect service quality or pricing

To exercise any right, contact our DPO at privacy@dealmind.ai. We respond within 30 days (72 hours for critical enterprise requests under SLA).

9. Data Retention

  • Active contract data: Retained during your subscription + 90 days post-termination
  • Hindsight Memory entries: Retained for the lifetime of your enterprise subscription; exported and deleted upon request
  • AI inference logs: Zero retention — purged immediately after processing
  • Usage analytics: Anonymized after 30 days; retained in aggregate form for 24 months
  • Support tickets: 3 years from resolution date
  • Billing records: 7 years (legal/tax compliance)
  • Security audit logs: 12 months (immutable)

10. International Data Transfers

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the EEA. We ensure such transfers are protected by:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) certification for U.S. transfers
  • Data Processing Agreements with all sub-processors
  • Regional data residency options: EU-West (Ireland), APAC (Singapore), US-East
